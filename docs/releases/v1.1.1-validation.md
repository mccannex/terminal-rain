## Known limits

Real X11, Intel Mac execution, multiple physical Mac displays and actual GPU resource loss remain untested. Do not add a Mac per-view fullscreen lease: ViewBridge Settings previews can be display-sized with `isPreview=NO`; that approach caused black fullscreen output and was removed. Duplicate protection is intentionally limited to Windows/Linux. Keep the separate SDL cleanup crash and unproven accumulation hypothesis distinct from the lease fix.
